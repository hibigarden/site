hibi

built around writing.

hibi gives you one fast place to write, edit, preview, and organize documents, with the flexibility of a code editor.

for authors.

write stories, essays, and notes. keep your drafts and research together in one workspace.

write papers in typst or latex with a preview beside your draft, and put equations right in your markdown notes.

for developers.

write documentation and export it as a site you can host. write and organize prompts, agent instructions, and context files for the ai tools you use.

open an obsidian vault where it is. link notes with [[wiki links]], tag them, and see how they connect on a graph. catch a thought with a quick note from any app.

frequently asked questions

is hibi free?

yes.

where are my notes stored?

your notes are saved as markdown files on your computer.

how do i enable addons?

open settings → addons and turn on the ones you want.

what stage is hibi in?

hibi is in beta.

is hibi stable?

bugs, slowdowns, and crashes can happen. addons are especially experimental and still being improved. if something breaks, please file an issue so i can keep track and work on a fix.

if you need a stable app, please check back later. this is a hobby project, so i can’t promise immediate fixes or new features. contributions and pull requests are always welcome.
