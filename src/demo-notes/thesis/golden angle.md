# golden angle

a sunflower packs its seeds by turning each new one the same angle from the last, the golden angle:

$$
\theta = 360^\circ \left(1 - \frac{1}{\varphi}\right) \approx 137.5^\circ
$$

where $\varphi = \frac{1 + \sqrt{5}}{2} \approx 1.618$ is the golden ratio.

seed $n$ sits at angle $n\theta$ and radius $r = c\sqrt{n}$, so no two seeds line up and the head fills without gaps. count the spirals and you usually get two fibonacci numbers in a row, like 34 and 55.
