# hibi landing page

## Register

brand

## Users

people looking for a free markdown editor for local notes and documentation.

## Product Purpose

introduce hibi with its actual editor components and concise feature descriptions. no public download URL is configured yet.

## Brand Personality

minimal, quiet, responsive. preserve the app's geist typography, neutral surfaces, compact controls, and teal accent.

## Anti-references

avoid decorative panels, excess borders, and distracting motion, following the app's product principles.

## Design Principles

- good markdown editors should be free.
- reuse hibi's original ui components and styles. do not approximate its editor with a mockup.
- use direct, factual copy. avoid decorative slogans and invented sample prose.
- make customization tangible.
- keep the page fast with static HTML and small, optional interactions.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

semantic landmarks, keyboard navigation, labeled controls, visible focus, readable contrast, and reduced-motion support.
