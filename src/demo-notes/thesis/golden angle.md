# golden angle

a sunflower packs its seeds by turning each new one the same angle from the last. the heads on the back fence do it, and so do the ones i left standing by the shed for the birds. the angle is the golden angle:

$$
\theta = 360^\circ \left(1 - \frac{1}{\varphi}\right) = \frac{360^\circ}{\varphi^2} \approx 137.5^\circ
$$

where $\varphi = \frac{1 + \sqrt{5}}{2} \approx 1.618$ is the golden ratio.

vogel's model puts seed $n$ at

$$
r_n = c\sqrt{n}, \qquad \alpha_n = n\,\theta
$$

so every seed gets the same area, no two seeds line up, and the head fills without gaps. count the spirals and you usually get two fibonacci numbers in a row, like 34 and 55.
