# chapter outline

working title: *the small blue*. one summer in one garden, told from the gate.

1. **may.** ada comes home to clear out her mother's house and finds the garden still running on its timer.
2. **the shed.** seed tins, gloves, the drip timer, and her mother's handwriting on every label.
3. **the apple tree.** a neighbour asks for the windfalls, and ada says no without knowing why.
4. **lavender.** the first small blue. she starts writing down what she sees, the way her mother did.
5. **september.** the sunflowers on the back fence go over. she leaves the seed heads for the birds.
6. **the gate.** october, one late butterfly, and the latch she finally fixes.

the story in [[the small blue]] is chapter six, written first so i know where everything is going.
