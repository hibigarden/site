frequently asked questions

is hibi free?

yes.

where are my notes stored?

your notes are saved as markdown files on your computer.

how do i enable addons?

open settings → addons and turn on the ones you want.

what stage is hibi in?

hibi is in beta.

is hibi stable?

bugs, slowdowns, and crashes can happen. addons are especially experimental and still being improved. if something breaks, please file an issue so i can keep track and work on a fix.

if you need a stable app, please check back later. this is a hobby project, so i can’t promise immediate fixes or new features. contributions and pull requests are always welcome.

how hibi compares.

apphow hibi differs
notion notion organizes pages and databases in a workspace. hibi edits documents directly in your local folders.
obsidian obsidian connects local markdown notes with links and plugins. hibi focuses on writing across document formats, with open source code.
typora typora focuses on markdown editing and live previews. hibi adds other document formats and writing tools through addons.
vscode vscode is built around software development. hibi brings a customizable editor to writing and working with documents.
word word focuses on formatted documents and collaboration. hibi gives you a customizable workspace for markdown and other text-based formats.
overleaf overleaf is an online latex editor built for collaboration. hibi is a local writing app for markdown, typst, and other document formats.
