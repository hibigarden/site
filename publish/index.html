hibi publish

turn a workspace into a website, for docs, notes or a digital garden, without leaving hibi.

what it does

  • publish from hibi

    choose publish workspace from the command palette. hibi asks you to confirm before anything is uploaded.

    publish
    Publish workspaceAddons
    ↑↓ Navigate↵ Runesc Close
    Publish workspace

    All pages in “garden” and their embedded media will become public at the configured site.

    CancelPublish workspace

  • built on the site export

    it publishes the site hibi’s export already makes, with your themes, navigation and search, and only swaps it in once the whole site checks out.

    gardenSearch
    welcomeplantsjournalreading
    welcome

  • roll back

    if a new version isn’t right, switch the site back to the one before it.

    gardenPublished
    https://pages.example.com/garden/
    Roll backReplace tokenRevoke

    Replace this site with its previous complete release?

    CancelOK

  • take it offline

    take a site down whenever you like. your workspace files are never touched.

    docsPublished
    https://pages.example.com/docs/
    Roll backRevoke
    gardenRevoked
    https://pages.example.com/garden/
    Restore access and issue token

what you can do today

choose export workspace to html from the command palette. you get a single html file, or a folder with a page per document (still experimental), and you can upload either to any web host. the export guide walks through the options.

questions

what becomes public?

every page and attachment in your export, so look through the export before you publish it.

do i need it to put my notes online?

no. hibi stays free, and the export already makes a site you can host anywhere.

when is it coming?

there’s no date yet.

hear about it first

publish will be announced in hibi’s discord and in the releases on github.
