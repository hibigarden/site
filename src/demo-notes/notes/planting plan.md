# planting plan

what goes where in spring, sunniest beds first. the drip lines follow this table, see [[watering]].

| plant     | bed             | sun   | water  | blooms            |
| --------- | --------------- | ----- | ------ | ----------------- |
| lavender  | by the gate     | full  | none   | june to august    |
| sunflower | back fence      | full  | medium | july to september |
| sweet pea | arch            | full  | high   | june to september |
| foxglove  | beside the shed | part  | medium | june to july      |
| hellebore | under the apple | shade | medium | february to april |

the sunflowers are the ones i measure for [[growth]].
